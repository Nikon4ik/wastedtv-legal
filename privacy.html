WastedTV Clips — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

WastedTV Clips is a private tool used only by its owner to upload video drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, username, display name) — only to confirm that the connected account is the intended one.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok — only to upload videos to the connected account's drafts.

How it is stored

  • Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, in a file that is never shared, published or sent to third parties.
  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

How it is used

  • Only to upload videos created by the owner to the owner's TikTok drafts. No advertising, no profiling, no selling or sharing of data.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Children

  • The app is not intended for and does not knowingly process data of children.

Changes and contact

  • This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. For questions, use the contact address listed on the TikTok for Developers app page.
