WastedTV Clips — Terms of Service

Last updated: November 18, 2026

WastedTV Clips is a private, non-commercial tool used only by its owner to prepare short video clips from live streams and upload them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts (@wastedtv.clips and @potrachen0.clips).

  • Who can use it. The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in with TikTok, and only to the owner's own accounts.
  • What it does. With your permission it reads your basic TikTok profile (to confirm the right account is connected) and uploads videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted publicly by the app: you review, edit and publish each draft yourself in the TikTok app.

  • Revoking access. You can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions).
